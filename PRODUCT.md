# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Consumers in India seeking healthcare — individuals and families researching doctors, hospitals, and health checkup packages, then booking appointments online. They arrive with a health concern or need in mind, often on mobile, and want to feel safe, informed, and confident before committing to a provider. Trust is the gate; convenience closes the deal.

## Product Purpose

MediHive is an integrated digital health clinic and storefront that connects consumers with doctors, hospitals, specialties, and health packages, and lets them book appointments in minutes. Success is a completed booking: every surface exists to reduce the distance from "I need care" to "my appointment is scheduled," without ever feeling transactional or rushed.

## Brand Personality

Calm. Confident. Human.

A brand that reassures rather than shouts. Quietly competent, clinically credible, and warm — the care feels human, never cold or sterile. It communicates certainty through restraint: clear hierarchy, honest language, and polished craft that signals "you can trust us with this."

## Anti-references

- **The generic AI health template**: the default SaaS-cream + teal + generic sans arrangement that most template-built health sites ship. No visual identity, no warmth, no point of view.
- **Cold clinical hospital look**: everything navy/cyan, sterile whites, clip-art medical icons. Walled-off, intimidating, and the opposite of "healthcare that feels human."
- The cramped AI default: -0.05em+ letter-spacing on display headings, over-rounded cards (24px+ radii), ghost-card borders paired with wide soft shadows.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through restraint** — visual calm and generous breathing room over decoration; the interface proves credibility by what it refuses to do.
2. **Booking is the destination** — every page funnels toward an appointment without pressure; the booking flow must feel frictionless end to end.
3. **Human before clinical** — warmth in language, imagery, and color; accessible, not sterile.
4. **One brand, everywhere** — the MediHive identity (name, tokens, voice) is consistent across every route; no drift, no one-off exceptions.
5. **Show, don't tell** — real doctors, real specialties, real proof (ratings, reviews, facilities) instead of marketing claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 against their backgrounds.
- Keyboard-first interactions with visible focus-visible states; semantic HTML and landmarks.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` respected — animations crossfade or skip, never gate content.
- Form inputs labeled, with descriptive error states and clear required indicators.
